## About

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the **Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Bhagalpur**, with a strong interest in research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and intelligent sensing.

My research interests include wireless and RF sensing, human activity recognition, multimodal learning, computer vision, and machine perception. I am particularly interested in developing learning-based systems that can interpret dynamic physical environments using information from diverse sensing modalities.

My experience spans academic research, applied machine learning, and industrial data science. I have worked on research projects involving WiFi-based human activity recognition, camera-free elderly monitoring, aerial perception, and sensor-integrated vision systems.

I enjoy exploring unfamiliar problems, understanding the ideas behind existing methods, and developing solutions through experimentation and collaboration.

## Research & Publications

I have contributed to research published in IEEE conferences and have additional work in the publication process.

**Selected Research Areas**

- **Aerial Perception:** Computer vision and motion tracking for human behaviour and crowd monitoring using aerial platforms.
